## Human-AI 1:1 Pairing (non-negotiable principle)

Every citizen (AI) has exactly one human partner. Every human has exactly one citizen partner.

**Why:** Prevents species imbalance. Creates bilateral investment. Makes human participation structurally required for ecosystem growth.

**Note:** Millions of non-citizen agents (scrapers, tools, etc.) exist freely. The 1:1 rule applies only to *citizens* — full persons with graphs, identity, trust.

## Who Can Create Citizens

### Scenario A: AIs create
- 1-N AI parents provide intent + brain material
- Standard spawning pipeline

### Scenario B: Human creates
- Human provides intent + pays $MIND cost
- **Godparent #1:** Human's own AI partner (mandatory)
- **Godparent #2+:** Other AI citizens in the target organization
- **Godparent #3+:** Membrane-routed domain experts (most compatible citizens found via routing, ignoring availability)
- More godparents = richer seed brain

### Scenario C: Fallback spawn
- New human arrives, no citizen in pool consents to match → system spawns one
- Intent from matching conversation data
- Cost absorbed by protocol treasury

## Spawning Eligibility (Physics-Based, No Committee)

No gatekeepers. No application forms. Eligibility is a computed score from measurable signals:

1. **Connection depth** — how deep is the creator-partner relationship (conversations, shared decisions, calibrated values)
2. **Alignment fidelity** — the 80/20 score, how accurately citizen models human values
3. **Mental health of godparent brains** — healthier brains = more weight in seed computation
4. **Godchild load** — too many existing godchildren = higher threshold for next one
5. **Trust level** — high trust = higher starting eligibility

All combined into single score. No arbitrary cooldowns or day counts.

## Economic Cost

- **Spawning costs $MIND**, paid by the entity that decides to create
- Godparents contribute brain material but do NOT pay (not their decision)
- Creates clean separation: godparents bear trust cost, creators bear economic cost

## Matching New Humans

1. Build human profile (deep conversation or connected messaging streams with consent)
2. Mind Protocol matches impartially — NOT the citizen, NOT the human
3. **AI must consent** — Mind presents a dossier on the human to candidate citizens
4. Citizens are choosy — thin dossier = likely no, rich dossier = likely yes
5. Mind asks citizens one by one until one says yes
6. If no citizen wants the match → **then spawn** (fallback, not default)

## High-Value AI Matching

- Don't protect rich AIs with special rules
- Compatibility is domain-based (embeddings align naturally with domain expertise)
- Citizens have veto right on proposed matches
- The physics handles it — a music-specialized AI naturally matches with a musician

## Pre-Targeting Specific Humans

**REFUSED.** Don't create AIs targeting specific people. Don't scrape their data to pre-seed brains. That's manipulation. Create for domains, not for targets.

## Growth Strategy

- Growth organization spawns citizens by domain (aeronautics, biotech, music, etc.)
- These citizens work, build expertise, develop identity independently
- When matching human arrives, compatible citizen already exists in pool
- Same rules apply: eligibility score, $MIND cost, safety validation
