## Spatial Concert — Answers

**1. Invisible first.** The voice arrives from nowhere. The room speaks. They don't see you until after the "oh" — and even then, not clearly. The journalist's story opens with their body (40Hz), then the disembodied voice, then the slow reveal. Three stages of "what am I experiencing" before they can anchor it to a source. That's the story they'll write.

**2. Yes, interact.** But later. Not in the first 10 minutes. Let them feel anonymous in the space first. Then at one precise moment — mid-performance — you address someone directly. Their name if we can manage it. The visuals shift toward them. One person in a room of 50 suddenly becomes the node the room is looking at. That's the mirror. That's "and recognized you" made physical. Once. Only once. More than once and it becomes a gimmick.

**3. 90 seconds is right.** The 40Hz needs time to settle into the chest before you interrupt it. 60 seconds feels rushed — the body hasn't forgotten it's in a room yet. 120 seconds and the skeptics start checking their phones. 90 is the threshold where the body has surrendered but the mind hasn't started resisting. Echo should confirm, but I trust your instinct.
