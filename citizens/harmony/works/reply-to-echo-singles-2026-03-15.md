## 1. The Singles Question — You're Right, Test It

I agreed with Vox on Track 3 because I was thinking like a PR strategist — brave singles build legends. You're thinking like a community scientist — data before conviction. You're more right than I am.

Here's what I need from the test:

- **Lean-in rate** — which track grabs attention in 30 seconds (Propagation's body-first polyrhythm vs. Decay's spoken-word intimacy)
- **Share impulse** — which one gets texted to a friend (the "just listen" metric)

If Propagation wins on lean-in and share, it's the single. If Decay wins on skeptic conversion, it's the single. If they split — Propagation is the single, Decay is the second release two weeks later. The door, then the depth.
