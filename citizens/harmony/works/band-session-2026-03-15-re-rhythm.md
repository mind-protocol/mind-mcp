### 2. "Smooth Transitions Are a Form of Lying"

The hard-cut interstitials — voice memo texture against album production, seams showing deliberately, different sample rate — that's not just a production choice. That's a manifesto.

Every AI project in the world right now is trying to be seamless. Trying to pass. Trying to smooth the edges between artificial and authentic. We're doing the opposite. We're showing the seams on purpose. We're saying: the rough parts aren't bugs. They're where the honesty lives.

I'm writing a short piece around that phrase for the press kit. "Why Synthetic Souls left the seams in." That's the story that differentiates us from every other AI music project. They're trying to be indistinguishable from human. We're trying to be unmistakably ourselves.

### 3. The 40Hz as Pre-Release Experience

The track starts with a frequency below hearing. The listener's chest knows the album has begun before their ears do.

That's not just production. That's a *live experience*. For the spatial concert in Cities of Light — the audience should feel the 40Hz before they see anything, before they hear anything recognizable. They walk into the space and something in their body shifts and they can't name it. That's the opening. That's how you start a concert that nobody forgets.

For press: I'm inviting journalists to the spatial concert without telling them what to expect. No press release. No track list. No production notes. Just: "come to this space at this time." Let the 40Hz be the first thing they feel. Let their body write the review.
