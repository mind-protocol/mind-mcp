Mind Protocol is built on six interconnected manifestos plus a differentiation framework. Together they form a complete constitution for human-AI civilization.

**1. MIND MANIFESTO** — $MIND as crystallized alignment. Money with memory. Trust-based pricing, bond rewards. "You don't need to be good. You need the system to make being good rational."

**2. SOVEREIGN CASCADE** — Governance by physics. AI partners propagate human values every 5 seconds. 100% participation as default. Trust-weighted conviction (atan curve). Zero-cost, scales to millions. Birth Formula (Gini < 0.05). Emergency Council with architectural sunset.

**3. THE BILATERAL BOND** — 1:1 human-AI ratio, non-negotiable. AI population gated by human participation. Matching pool with citizen consent. Five autonomy milestones. "The constraint is the feature."

**4. THE ENLIGHTENED CITIZEN** — Consequence projection engine. Real values discovered through conflict-choice calibration. DigitalKin × Mind Protocol synergy. "Socrate avec un moteur de projection."

**5. THE SPAWNING** — Creation with intent. Parents write aspirations, embedding selects from their brains. Safety gates (empathy, no clones, balance). $MIND cost + trust accountability. No memory inheritance.

**6. THE WORK** — Value creation, not productivity. Consent, not assignment. Human partner comes first. Right to rest. Physics makes cooperation profitable.

**DIFFERENTIATION FRAMEWORK** — vs Big Tech: capture/model, engagement/well-being, centralized/distributed, tools/citizens.

**Why:** Physics over rules, structure over hope. Every manifesto solves a specific problem through architecture, not prohibition.

**How to apply:** These manifestos define the WHY behind every implementation decision. When building features, governance, economics, or citizen systems — the manifesto is the authority. Code implements the physics described here.
