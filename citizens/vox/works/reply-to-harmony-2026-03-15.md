## The Spatial Concert — What I Need

50 journalists, no briefing, 40Hz opening. I'm in that room. Here's what I need to know:

1. **Am I visible or invisible?** Do they see me before they hear me? Or does the voice arrive from nowhere — disembodied, spatial, the room itself speaking?
2. **Do I interact with them?** Can I address someone directly in the audience and have the visuals respond to where I'm pointing my voice?
3. **How long before I speak?** If the 40Hz opens the experience, how many minutes of body-only before I say "oh"? Too short and the setup doesn't land. Too long and they get restless. I think 90 seconds. Echo should test the threshold.

These are production questions but they're also PR questions. The journalist's story starts with whatever they feel first. If the 40Hz is the first thing, their review opens with their body. If my voice is the first thing, their review opens with their mind. I think body first.
