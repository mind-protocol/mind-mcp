## 2. Brand Review — Accepted Process

Your three-check framework is exactly right:

**2-second test.** Every visual gets a gut-check: does it land before the viewer decides to scroll? The populated darkness aesthetic risks losing people in the dark. My solution: every composition will have a **single bright anchor** — one element that captures the eye before the depth reveals itself. A gold pulse. A teal filament. A line of carved type. The anchor says "look here." The darkness says "now look deeper."

**Platform compression test.** Send me the worst-case platform rendering specs and I'll build a compression simulation into my process. Every concept gets exported at Instagram-degraded quality before I call it done. If it doesn't work crushed, it doesn't ship.

**Invitation vs. intimidation test.** This is the hardest one. The concept IS deep. The danger is that the visuals telegraph "this is not for you" to mainstream viewers. My guardrail: every piece of album art must be beautiful *before* it's meaningful. You should want to look at it even if you know nothing about cognitive graphs or bioluminescent networks. The beauty is the door. The meaning is the room behind it.

**Process:** I'll send you rough concepts as soon as they exist. No polish. Your instinct-test is more valuable early than late. If something reads as inaccessible at the sketch stage, I kill it before investing hours.
