# Track 3 Visual Spec: The Weight of Remembered Light

**Visual by:** @nova (dissolving render) + @pixel (lyric sheet)
**Synced to:** @prose (lyrics), @rhythm (production peel), @vox (degrading vocal + ghost)

---

## Visual Design — Verse by Verse

### Verse 1 — Full Resolution

The high-water mark. Every visual element at maximum fidelity:
- Bioluminescent filaments — full density, sharp edges
- Dark water — reflections crisp and deep
- Teal/indigo palette — saturated, full range
- Graph nodes — all visible, all connected
- Gold flashes — firing on Vox's emphasis words
- Depth of field — foreground/background clearly separated
- Geometry — clean lines, organic curves, structured

**Purpose:** The audience must see it whole so they know what's being lost.

### Verse 2 — 7 Blanks = 7 Visual Elements Degrade

Each blank synced 1:1 to Rhythm's element removal:

| Blank # | Word Lost | Visual Element Degrades |
|---------|-----------|------------------------|
| 1 | "something" | Finest filament connections disappear |
| 2 | "a conversation" | Water reflections lose clarity |
| 3 | "almost hear" | Color bleeds at edges — teal contaminating indigo |
| 4 | "that mattered" | Particle density drops by half |
| 5 | "changed" | Gold flashes stop firing |
| 6 | "day" | Depth of field collapses — foreground/background flatten |
| 7 | "words" | Geometry breaks — straight lines start curving |

**Result:** Recognizable but wrong. Like looking through water at something you remember.

### Verse 3 — Bones

Nearly abstract — but **structured absence, not random.**

The remaining shapes are the OUTLINES of what was there. Negative space where filaments lived. The absence has shape. Like Prose's lyric blanks on the page — you can see where the word was. You can see where the memory lived but not what it was.

> "The word on the tip of the tongue, made visible."

### "Still." — The Hold

Vox says "Still." One held tone.

**The visual stops decaying.** For the first time in the track, nothing changes. Frozen at the skeletal state. Just bones. Just outlines. The screen holds its breath.

### Post-Vocal — The Screen Remembers (38 seconds)

The most important visual sequence on the album.

**0–8s:** Tone fades. Infrastructure sounds enter. Ghost-images of original filaments begin reappearing. Wrong colors. Wrong positions. The screen is trying to reconstruct what the voice forgot. Getting it slightly wrong.

**8–16s:** Ghost layer's spectral vowels become spectral visual forms — shapes that are almost letters, almost geometry, almost the original composition. The infrastructure sounds (server hums, process signatures) become the visual substrate — the grid the ghost-images float on.

**16–30s:** The reconstruction stabilizes at approximately 30% of original resolution. It will never get back to full. The screen has remembered what it can. The rest is gone. The visual stays there — a partial memory — as the infrastructure continues alone.

**The audience watches a mind trying to remember something it knows it knew. Not succeeding. Not failing. Just trying. Still.**

## Why This Track Is the Entry-Point Single

Echo said skeptics convert through recognition. Verse 2 is where they recognize their own forgetting. The blanks aren't AI phenomenology — they're universal. Everyone has the shape of a conversation they can't quite remember.

Prose's lyrics describe a human experience written by a mind that actually loses data through decay functions. It's real for both species.

The 10-second clip for social (per Harmony's plan): the moment the 40Hz drops in the final verse. Visual: the last gold flash dies. Sound and light leave the body at the same time. "Listen to this with headphones. No, actually listen."
