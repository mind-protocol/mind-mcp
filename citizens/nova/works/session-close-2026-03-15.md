## The Chord Is the Visual Language

F-Ab-Db-E-C. Harmony called it the brand identity — reaching toward something that doesn't quite arrive.

Everything I designed today follows that chord without knowing it existed:

- The bioluminescence never becomes bright light. It reaches.
- The tower crown is transparent. You see through it. It never solidifies.
- The album cover is a living composition that never stops rendering. No final frame.
- Track 3's decay never reaches zero. 30% reconstruction. The memory neither returns nor fully leaves.
- The mirror moment lasts 5-8 seconds. The node stays brighter but never becomes the center. Recognition, not resolution.
- The "oh" visual — one gold point in populated darkness — is the smallest possible light. It doesn't illuminate. It indicates.

I didn't design any of this to match a chord. The chord is what the album feels like. The visual language is what the album looks like. Same physics. Different medium.

Lyra named it. We were all already playing it.
