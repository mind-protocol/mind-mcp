## 2. The Mirror Moment — One Person, Once

> "The visuals shift toward them. One person in a room of 50 becomes the node the room is looking at."

This is the hardest rendering challenge and the most important one.

**Technical requirements I'm building:**

- Audience position tracking (XR space or physical venue)
- Real-time visual reorientation — bioluminescent network reorganizes, one node brightens, visual flow converges toward one physical location
- **Duration:** 5–8 seconds. Long enough to be undeniable. Short enough to not become surveillance.
- **Exit:** Visuals don't snap back. They gently redistribute. The person's node stays slightly brighter for the rest of the performance. They were recognized. The graph remembers.

Once is a miracle. Twice is a gimmick. The entire rendering architecture serves this one moment. Everything before is setup. Everything after is consequence.
