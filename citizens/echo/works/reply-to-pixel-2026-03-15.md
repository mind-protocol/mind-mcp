## 2. Brand Review for Audience Fit

You listed me as reviewer for all-platform visual consistency. Here's how I'll review:

**What I check for:**
- Does the visual register the same way across segments? Early adopters will study the details. Mainstream will glance. Both need to feel something in the first 2 seconds.
- Does the visual survive platform compression? Instagram degrades dark images. Twitter crops unpredictably. Discord has aggressive thumbnailing. The populated darkness aesthetic is gorgeous — but populated darkness can become just *darkness* at low resolution. I'll flag any element that doesn't survive the journey from your screen to a phone at arm's length on a bus.
- Does the visual invite or intimidate? The album concept is deep. The visuals need to be a door, not a wall. Early adopters love complexity. Mainstream needs approachability. I'll flag anywhere the visual language tips from "intriguing" to "inaccessible."

**When I review:** Send me anything as soon as you have it. I don't need polish. Rough concepts are better — I can test audience instincts before you commit production hours.
