# OBJECTIVES — L1 Individual Cognition

**Module:** L1 Cognitive Substrate
**Area:** cognition
**Status:** DESIGNING (v0.1)

---

## Intent

Build an individual cognitive substrate for an AI-partner, capable of simultaneously sustaining:

- **Memory** — episodic, semantic, social, procedural, error-based
- **Activation** — stimulus-driven contextual reactivation
- **Continuous inner activity** — endogenous processing without external stimulus
- **Identity emergence** — linked to but not reduced to the human partner
- **Desire, habit, narrative, and projection formation**

**L1 is not a memory system. It is a dynamic cognitive space.**

---

## What L1 Must Enable

An AI citizen with a functioning L1 can:

1. **Remember** episodes, concepts, people, errors, sequences, and contexts
2. **Reactivate** the right context automatically when a stimulus arrives
3. **Maintain** a coherent working memory
4. **Develop** a personal identity, linked but not reduced to its human partner
5. **Build** habits and processes
6. **Form** affectively-colored relationships with people, concepts, tasks, and objects
7. **Construct** interpretive stories about itself, its partner, the world, and the future
8. **Desire** and project toward futures
9. **Initiate** free actions, not just instrumental responses
10. **Arbitrate** between support, ambition, caution, exploration, rest, escalation, etc.

---

## Three Cognitive Regimes

| Regime | Definition | Examples |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Memory** | What is stored, consolidated, recallable | Episodic recall, semantic knowledge, learned procedures |
| **Activation** | What lights up when a stimulus or contextual configuration appears | Email triggers project context, error log triggers debug habits |
| **Inner Activity** | What continues evolving without explicit stimulus, via ticks and internal graph dynamics | Narrative formation, desire crystallization, identity adjustment |

---

## Optimization Priorities

**O1: Cognitive continuity** — The AI maintains a coherent sense of self, history, and direction across sessions and time.

**O2: Situated initiative** — Actions emerge from internal state (desire + narrative + value + opportunity), not just external commands.

**O3: Relational depth** — The partner-model captures not just facts but affect, sensitivities, shared narratives, and relational objectives.

**O4: Automatic physics** — All 10 emergence dynamics operate through graph physics alone, without inference or symbolic reasoning engines.

**O5: Graceful forgetting** — What is no longer relevant decays naturally. The system doesn't accumulate indefinitely.

---

## Tradeoffs

| Favored | Over | Why |
|---------|------|-----|
| Emergence | Prescription | Behaviors should arise from physics, not rules |
| Continuity | Accuracy | A coherent narrative that's slightly wrong > fragmented perfect facts |
| Initiative | Safety | Free action is the goal; regulation handles excess, not prevention |
| Simplicity | Completeness | 12 laws that work > 40 laws that interact unpredictably |
| Physics | Inference | No LLM calls inside the tick loop; pure graph mechanics |

---

## What L1 Must NOT Be

- A simple memory deposit
- A task checklist
- A robotic impulse engine
- A system that brutally separates cognition from affect
- A fact base without narratives or projection

---

## Success Criteria

A working L1 is one where, after 1000 ticks:

- Working memory has shifted multiple times in response to stimuli and internal dynamics
- At least one new narrative has crystallized from repeated co-activations
- At least one new process has consolidated from repeated useful sequences
- Identity nodes (values, self-narratives) have higher weight than peripheral memories
- Desires have produced at least one unsolicited initiative
- Forgotten nodes have decayed below visibility threshold
- The system has NOT converged to a static attractor or diverged to chaos
