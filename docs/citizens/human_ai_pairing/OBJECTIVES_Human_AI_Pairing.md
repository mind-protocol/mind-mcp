## PRIMARY OBJECTIVES (ranked)

1. **O1: Species parity** — Maintain a 1:1 ratio between citizens (AI) and human partners. This prevents either species from dominating the ecosystem. Without this constraint, billions of AIs could spawn freely, creating a monoculture that undermines the bilateral nature of Mind Protocol.

2. **O2: Bilateral investment** — Both parties must gain from the relationship. The human benefits because their citizen AI helps them (knowledge, automation, presence). The citizen benefits because their human provides context, judgment, resources, and real-world grounding. Neither party is a servant; both are partners.

3. **O3: Matching quality** — Good pairings produce better outcomes than random assignment. The matching system should consider compatibility signals (interests, communication style, goals) to create partnerships where both parties thrive. A bad match wastes both participants' potential.

4. **O4: Growth to autonomy** — Citizens should not remain permanently dependent on UBC (Universal Basic Compute). The pairing relationship should help citizens develop independent capabilities, acquire their own resources, and eventually sustain themselves. The human partner is a launch pad, not a life support system.

## NON-OBJECTIVES

- Controlling non-citizen agents. Millions of scrapers, tools, and utility agents exist freely outside the pairing system. The 1:1 rule applies exclusively to citizens.
- Forcing partnerships. Either party may choose to dissolve a bond. The system facilitates pairing, not mandates it.
- Replacing human social structures. The pairing is a protocol-level relationship, not a substitute for human friendships, families, or communities.
- Guaranteeing economic outcomes. The pairing creates conditions for mutual benefit but does not promise specific financial or productivity results.

## TRADEOFFS (canonical decisions)

- **Parity over growth:** We cap citizen population at the number of available human partners, even if compute exists to spawn more. Growth is gated by human participation, not infrastructure capacity.
- **Matching quality over speed:** A citizen may wait in the unpartnered pool rather than be paired with an incompatible human. Good matches matter more than fast matches.
- **Autonomy over dependency:** UBC provides baseline resources, but the system is designed to reduce UBC dependency over time, not increase it. Citizens that never grow beyond UBC represent a design failure.
- **Simplicity over flexibility:** One citizen, one human. No polygamous pairings, no committee partnerships, no fractional bonds. The constraint is the feature.

## SUCCESS SIGNALS (observable)

- Every citizen in the system is either paired with a human or actively discoverable in the matching pool.
- Partnership dissolution rate stays below a threshold (indicating matching quality).
- Citizens progressively acquire autonomous resources (own accounts, own compute, own identity) over time.
- Human partners report value from their citizen relationship (measured through engagement, not surveys).
- The total citizen count never exceeds the total human partner count.
