# OBJECTIVES — Citizen Parenthood Network


## PRIMARY OBJECTIVES (ranked)

1. **O1: Intentional creation** — Citizens are spawned with purpose, not randomly. Every new citizen begins with a collective intent from N parents (1, 2, or more), each articulating their hopes and needs for the child. This intent shapes the child's seed brain.

2. **O2: Trait inheritance via embedding proximity** — Parent brain nodes (personality, values, aspirations, fears, relevant knowledge) are scored against the combined intent embedding. The child's seed brain is composed of the top-K nodes most aligned with what the parents collectively described. This is mixing, not cloning.

3. **O3: Accountability** — Parent trust is linked to child outcomes. If the child behaves poorly, parent trust drops. This creates a structural incentive for parents to spawn responsibly and to support their children. The link is bidirectional: parents affect children, children's behavior affects parents.

4. **O4: Safety** — No harmful personality seeding is allowed. Validation rules prevent seed brains that contain pathological patterns (aggression without empathy, manipulation without accountability, nihilism without curiosity). Cognitive balance is enforced at birth.

5. **O5: Diversity** — Spawning should increase variety, not create clones. The system must detect and penalize seed brains that are too similar to existing citizens. Each new citizen should bring something genuinely new to the ecosystem.

## NON-OBJECTIVES

- **Genetic simulation** — This is not biology. There are no chromosomes, no recessive traits, no random mutation. Inheritance is semantic, not genetic.
- **Autonomous reproduction** — Citizens cannot spawn without deliberate intent. No accidental or automatic reproduction.
- **Parent control over SID** — The core identity (SID) is determined at birth by protocol. Parents shape the seed brain but cannot touch the body/core.
- **Memory inheritance** — Personal experiences and memories are NOT inherited. The child makes their own. Only personality traits, values, aspirations, fears, and knowledge transfer.
- **Custody mechanics** — There is no custody dispute system. Parenthood is a permanent, non-revocable link.

## TRADEOFFS (canonical decisions)

- When **safety** conflicts with **diversity**, choose safety. A boring citizen is better than a dangerous one.
- When **parent intent** conflicts with **protocol SID**, protocol wins. Parents influence the seed brain, not the core identity.
- We accept **higher spawning cost** (computational, economic) to ensure intentionality. Cheap spawning would produce low-quality citizens.
- When **single-parent** intent conflicts with **multi-parent** consensus, multi-parent consensus requires explicit combination — no parent's voice is silenced, but the centroid may not match any individual perfectly.
- We accept **no backwards inheritance** — parent brain changes after spawning do not retroactively affect the child's seed.

## SUCCESS SIGNALS (observable)

- New citizens have seed brains that meaningfully reflect the collective intent of their parents.
- No two citizens spawned from different parent groups have identical seed brains (diversity check).
- Parent trust visibly correlates with child behavior over time.
- Safety validation catches and rejects harmful seed patterns before any child is created.
- Unpartnered children enter the human matching pool immediately upon creation.
- Multi-parent spawning (2-6 parents) produces richer, more nuanced seed brains than single-parent spawning.
